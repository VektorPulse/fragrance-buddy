How it works

Three steps, and you never leave the store you're on

Step 1

Tell it what you like

Six quick questions about who you shop for, when you wear fragrance and your budget. Then twelve sample scents: tap the notes that sound good, like vanilla, bergamot or oud, and skip the ones that don't. Add bottles you already love if you want.

Step 2

Shop the way you already do

Open any fragrance page. A small button appears in the corner and every bottle gets a match score, right on the store's own tiles. Sort by best match, lowest price or best value per millilitre.

93% match
Step 3

Get a straight answer on any bottle

On a product page you see a clear verdict, the top, heart and base notes, how long it lasts, how far it projects, whether it fits your budget, and cheaper alternatives that smell similar.

What you get

Fewer blind buys you regret

"Has notes you picked: tobacco, vanilla, lavender." You always see why something matched.

Real prices

Sale prices, price ranges and sizes are read correctly, with price per millilitre so a 3.4 oz and a 1.7 oz bottle compare fairly.

Filters that fit fragrance

Price range, concentration from cologne to extrait, minimum longevity, and whether to show gift sets, minis and samples.

A library of about 140 scents

Designer classics, niche favorites and budget picks, each with notes, accords, longevity and projection.

Gets better as you go

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. The quiz, matching, filters and badges are free.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome on a computer. It also installs from the Chrome Web Store in other Chromium browsers such as Edge, Brave and Opera. It doesn't run on phones, since mobile Chrome doesn't support extensions.

Does it smell things for me?

No. It compares each fragrance's notes, accords, longevity and projection with what you told it you like. That's why it's good at narrowing a page of 70 bottles down to the five worth sampling.

What if a fragrance isn't recognized?

Blind Buy still reads the price and size. You can add it to your favorites by hand, or turn on the optional AI lookup to identify fragrances outside the built-in library.

Is Blind Buy connected to these stores?

No. It's an independent tool and isn't affiliated with any retailer or fragrance brand. Brand and product names belong to their owners.
